## ⬇️ [Download Heron for Mac](https://github.com/andrew-c-lo/Heron/releases/latest/download/Heron.zip)

Unzip it and drag Heron to your Applications folder, over the old one: your macros, settings and permissions carry over. It runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 14 or later. (`Heron.zip` and `Heron-1.2.1.zip` below are the same file.)

**First launch:** macOS blocks it once, because Heron isn't notarized by Apple ($99 a year; Heron is free and open source). Open it, click **Done**, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. [Step by step](https://github.com/andrew-c-lo/Heron#install).

**Background taps in iPhone Mirroring, a lot of fixes, and one place for time limits.**

### New

- **In the background now works with iPhone Mirroring.** Heron taps your phone while you keep using your Mac: Mirroring isn't brought forward, and your keyboard stays with the app you're in.

### Fixed

- **Words match whole words.** A step looking for “Claim” no longer clicks “Claimed”, and “Collect” no longer clicks “Collected”. Phrases and languages written without spaces still match inside a line.
- **Round goals count correctly.** In All at once, a button that flickered or reacted to the click could be counted twice, so “stop after 6” sometimes stopped early.
- **Background clicks work in standard Mac apps.** They used to miss the button and land on the window's title bar.
- **A macro with a setting this version doesn't know** (from a newer Heron, or a damaged file) still loads: only that setting falls back, instead of the macro disappearing from the list.
- **Type from a list types every character**, including ones with no key on your keyboard layout (é, emoji, Chinese), instead of dropping them.
- **A number reaching** reads “30/50” as 30 (not 50), “1.5k” as 1500, and full-width digits.
- **Recording into a macro** adds after its steps instead of replacing them.
- Deleting the step that “Stop after N times” counts removes that stop instead of leaving one that never fires.
- Pressing Play on a macro whose steps are all switched off says so, instead of looping on nothing.
- Imported macros: files that aren't macros are refused, and imported schedules stay off until you turn them on.
- A shortcut that's switched off for being a standard Mac shortcut (like ⌘Q) no longer shows on the Play button.
- The schedule's settings no longer show while the schedule is off.
- The green run dot sits centred in its ring.

### Better

- **Stops › After a set time** ends any run after a set time, whether you started it or its schedule did. It replaces Playback's “For a duration” and the schedule's own time limit (existing ones move over by themselves). Turning on a schedule for a macro that never stops on its own starts it at 30 minutes.
- **Pictures without words get a colour name**: “Tap the red picture” instead of “Tap the picture”.
- **Macro menu shortcuts**: ⌘1 Target, ⌘2 Playback, ⌘3 Stops, ⌘4 Schedule. **⌥⌘I** hides the step details panel so the list gets the whole width.
- **Help inside the app** (Help › Heron Help), and tips in the details panel when no step is selected.
- **“Can't find a window”** messages, and a target that closes mid-run, come with a button that opens the app; a scheduled run that can't start sends a notification saying why.
- Time limits read “30 min” or “1 h 30 min”; a schedule with no days says “No days chosen”; a macro name can't be left empty.
- The live strip names what it's watching for (“Looking for “Claim””), and the menu bar shows time left or time so far for any run.
- Settings chips stay readable at every window size, and turn blue when they're set.
