**Heron now sets itself up, runs on its own, and stops when the job is done.** Your macros, settings and permissions carry over: install it over the old app.

**Download `Heron-1.2.0.zip` below**, unzip it, and drag Heron to your Applications folder. It runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 14 or later. The first time, open it once, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**, because Heron isn't notarized by Apple.

### New

- **Templates.** A new macro asks what it should do: click something whenever it appears, do steps in order, run on a schedule, or repeat until it's done.
- **Schedules.** Start a macro daily at set times, every few hours, or when its app opens, with a time limit and a notification when it's done. Optionally open Heron at login.
- **Stop when it appears.** A picture or some words that end the run as soon as they show up.
- **Keep the screen on** while a macro runs, so the screen saver and auto-lock don't stop it.
- **Smarter recording.** Every tap becomes a step that finds what you tapped, by its picture, its words or both. Quick repeated taps become “keep tapping until it's gone”.
- **Picture, words or both** in one step, and **Spot** to click fixed coordinates instead, switching back anytime.
- **Type from a list**: each run types the next item and remembers where it got to.
- **Resizes with the window**: pictures, areas and positions follow a bigger or smaller window.
- **Click boxes** spread clicks over the whole box; the wait before clicking can be a random range; and **Vary the timing** varies the waits between steps.
- **Waits for things to stop moving** before clicking.
- **Suggestions from your own taps** while a macro plays.
- **Reorder steps** by dragging, from the right-click menu, or with ⌥⌘↑ and ⌥⌘↓.
- **A cleaner editor**: one toolbar, an Add menu with every kind of step, fine-tuning under More options, and a dot on every macro to start or stop it.

### Better

- **Much faster picture matching**: large pictures that took seconds now take milliseconds.
- **More reliable words**: short words like “1” match only on their own, and text in a search area is read in context.
- **Jump & return is the default**; the old Normal delivery is gone.
- Search-area suggestions learn from past runs, and layout fixes at every window size.
