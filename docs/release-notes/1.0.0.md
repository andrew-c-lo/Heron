The first release of MacroClicker: a free Mac auto clicker that can see your screen.

**Download `MacroClicker-1.0.0.zip` below**, unzip it, and drag MacroClicker to your Applications folder. It runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

### Opening it the first time

MacroClicker isn't notarized by Apple (that needs a paid developer account), so macOS asks before opening it the first time:

1. Open MacroClicker. macOS says it can't check it for malicious software. Click **Done**.
2. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down to “MacroClicker was blocked”, and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Open MacroClicker again and confirm.

You only do this once. Then allow the permissions it asks for: **Accessibility** to click and type, **Input Monitoring** to record, and optionally **Screen Recording** to find pictures and words.

### What's in it

- **Finds pictures and words** in any window and clicks them the moment they appear. Words are read on your Mac.
- **Record and build macros** with one-click steps (Click, Type, Wait, Find Picture, Find Text), and see a recording as a map of taps and swipes.
- **Background macros** that keep clicking pop-ups whenever they show up while you do other things.
- **A proper auto clicker** with a speed readout, hotkeys and a small Simple mode that stays on top.
- **Private**: no account, no analytics, no network code.
