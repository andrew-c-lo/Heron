**MacroClicker is now Heron**, with a new icon. Your macros, settings and permissions carry over: install it over the old app and it picks up where you left off.

**Download `Heron-1.1.0.zip` below**, unzip it, and drag Heron to your Applications folder. It runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 14 or later. The first time, open it once, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**, because Heron isn't notarized by Apple.

### New

- **Remember what you click.** Recorded clicks on a word or an icon find it wherever it is when played back, and click the recorded spot if it isn't there.
- **Chains that recover on their own.** Tap when stuck taps a spot you choose when nothing shows up for a while, and keeps that screen under Stuck Screens so you can turn it into a step.
- **Higher steps win**, as an option when several things are on screen at once.
- **Run statistics**: how often each step fired, and a suggested search area from where it was found.
- **Branching and counters**: go to another step when something isn't found, and repeat from an earlier step a set number of times.
- **Background macros** replace watchers: any macro can run alongside others with its own switch. Old watchers convert automatically.
- **A new macro editor**: one-click Add buttons and a details panel for the selected step.
- **On-device help** with Apple Intelligence (macOS 26 or later): Describe drafts steps from a sentence, Suggest picks the word to tap on a stuck screen, and Autopilot (experimental) taps toward a goal.
- **Folders** for macros, a **Simple mode** that stays on top, and a hotkey for **Double-click everywhere**.
- **Faster** reading of words in chains, and many layout fixes across window sizes.
