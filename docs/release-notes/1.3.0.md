## ⬇️ [Download Heron for Mac](https://github.com/andrew-c-lo/Heron/releases/latest/download/Heron.zip)

Unzip it and drag Heron to your Applications folder, over the old one: your macros, settings and permissions carry over. It runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 14 or later. (`Heron.zip` and `Heron-1.3.0.zip` below are the same file.)

**First launch:** macOS blocks it once, because Heron isn't notarized by Apple ($99 a year; Heron is free and open source). Open it, click **Done**, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. [Step by step](https://github.com/andrew-c-lo/Heron#install).

**If steps, a mini mode for macros, Android emulators, a macro check-up, and a stuck tap that knows when to stop.**

### New

- **If, Otherwise and End.** Run some steps only when a picture or words are (or aren't) on screen, a number has reached a value, a spot is a colour, or it's a certain run. Select steps and choose **Add › If…** to wrap them.
- **Run another macro** as a step: its steps play in place, then the macro carries on.
- **Mini mode for macros.** One small card you can drag anywhere, swipe between macros, play or stop, and see the run count. It stays on top (the pin turns that off), and switches to the auto clicker, where **Set Spot** picks a new spot.
- **Count runs.** Pick the step that happens once per run (an “Attempt again” button, say): Heron counts it, ignoring double presses, and shows the count from 0. **− and +** next to the count set how many runs to stop after, even mid-run.
- **Macro › Check Macro… (⌘5)** tries each picture on screens saved from the macro, at a few window sizes, and lists settings that can't work as saved, with the fix: a strictness its own picture can't meet (with a safe value to use), one so low a look-alike nearly passes, a picture picked in another app or window, or a search area somewhere else than the picture.
- **Android emulators (BlueStacks).** Background taps go in over ADB, so the pointer never moves. **Target › Read the screen › From Android** reads the Android screen at full resolution, so the window's size, its sidebar and anything covering it don't matter.

### Better

- **Tap when stuck** doesn't tap blind on a screen showing prices, stops the run when two taps in a row leave the screen unchanged, and only counts as stuck when neither Heron nor you have done anything: your clicks, typing, scrolling or moving the pointer over the window all count.
- **All at once** tries a click again if the button is still there, unchanged, a few seconds later (it was missed), but not if the click visibly worked.
- **Picture matching is about 3× faster**, and Heron looks at the screen at most 10 times a second: a long macro uses far less CPU.
- **Resized windows** keep working: each picture and search area remembers the window size it was picked in, and emulator windows follow their Android screen (sidebar shown or not).
- **New Macro** is now the main button in the list: it starts from what you want the macro to do, or records it.
- **Background** is the default for macros with an app.
- The **map view** marks each exact spot with a ring and its number, so the buttons underneath stay visible.
- Heron reopens on the tab you left it on.

### Fixed

- A step found by its words still clicks inside its click box, not on the words.
- Text steps in order no longer miss their words between reads.
- A search area drawn tighter than its picture could never match.
- Mini mode no longer opens off-screen after a display is unplugged.
- A small floating window of an app could be taken for its main window.

### Learning from your runs (stays on your Mac)

Heron keeps the screens it gets stuck on and what got it going again (your own tap, a step firing by itself, or you stopping it), match scores, and a sample screen every 90 seconds while a macro runs. It uses them to check macros, and to test a learned classifier and Apple's on-device model on stuck screens without letting them act (**Settings › Learn from stuck screens**). Nothing leaves your Mac.
